# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and be kind. Disagree about the work, not about the person.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space the
project uses.

## Expected behavior

- **Argue from evidence.** This is a measurement framework. "I think X is
  faster" and "here is a bundle showing X is faster" are different
  contributions, and the second is the one that settles things.
- **Assume the other person ran into something real.** A confusing error, a
  number that will not reproduce, a document that is wrong: these are reports
  about the project, not about the reporter's competence.
- **Say when you are unsure.** A question asked early costs less than a wrong
  number published late.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, or sustained disruption.
Publishing someone's private information. Deliberately submitting results you
know to be misattributed or fabricated.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns through the repository's issue tracker if the matter is
public, or to the maintainers privately if it is not. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers who do not follow this code are subject to it in the
same way as everyone else.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions that do not follow this
code, and may temporarily or permanently block accounts for repeated or severe
violations. Where the situation allows it, a warning and a chance to correct
comes first.
